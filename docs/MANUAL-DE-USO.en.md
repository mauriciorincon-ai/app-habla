# Hablemos San — User manual (English)

> For the parent who uses the app with their child. Plain language, no jargon. Written in English, not
> translated line by line: the Spanish manual (`MANUAL-DE-USO.md`) covers the same features.

## What this app is

**Hablemos San** answers one question every day: _"what can I do with my child today?"_ Each day it offers one
technique with research behind it, explained in 30 seconds, with **one concrete line you can say today** and an
activity to do together. Some days the activity includes a game where **your child's voice moves a balloon**.

It is **home practice that encourages speech**. It is not therapy, it does not assess anyone, and it makes no
promises about when anything will happen. It sits beside — never in place of — your child's own speech and
language therapy.

**The app guides the adult; it does not replace the adult.** What moves a child's speech along is time with you.
The screen is only the excuse to play together.

## First steps

1. Open the app in the browser of your tablet or phone (Chrome on Android works well).
2. **Install it as an app** (optional, recommended): in the browser menu, "Add to home screen". It then opens
   full screen and works offline.
3. The first time, it asks two things: what your child is called at home (optional) and up to three topics they
   like. **All of it stays on the device**; there are no accounts and no sign-up.
4. Want to change the nickname or topics later? **Settings → "Nickname and topics"**. Changing them erases
   nothing: progress stays as it is.

## Language: Español or English

The app speaks **Spanish and English**, and each language is written for that language. By default it **follows
your browser's language**. To pick the other one, open **Settings → "Idioma / Language"** and tap **Español** or
**English** (the chosen one shows a «✓»). The change is immediate, needs no reload, and stays on the device like
any other setting.

- **What changes:** Today, the daily capsule (all 50), first use, the games, the Studio, the Goal, the Path,
  Settings, and the brochure page `/conoce`, which has its own switch.
- **The daily capsule is the same** in both languages: switching language never changes what is on for today.
- **Words and Twins, in English:** those two games practise Spanish words and pictures, so the app says so on a
  plain card instead of pretending. The voice games (balloon and rocket) play the same in both.
- **The weekly Goal, in English:** the screen reads in English, but the aligner and its dictionary still
  understand Spanish words, and the card says so.
- **Privacy:** switching language sends nothing anywhere.

## How to use it

### Today — your daily answer

On opening you see **the capsule of the day**: the technique, why it works, **your line for today**, and the
activity. When you have done it, the app asks "Did you do today's activity?" and you tap **"Yes, we did it"**.

- Each day brings a different capsule; none repeats until all have been used.
- Today's capsule **does not change** if you close and reopen the app.
- At the bottom it counts how many days you have practised together **in total**. There are **no streaks and no
  penalties**: the number never goes down. If you skip a day, nothing is lost. What counts is coming back.
- Under "Where does this come from?" you can see the study each capsule rests on.

### The voice games — "their voice moves the world"

On Today there is a permanent card, **"Voice games"**. Open it and choose among **four games**. None asks the
child for words: they respond to voice (or taking part), not vocabulary.

**Three of them listen to the microphone** (balloon, rocket, and word and picture). Before any of those:

1. **Your line first.** The app shows the line you will say. You lead; the child does not play alone (there is
   no "child alone" mode, on purpose).
2. **Microphone permission.** The browser asks whether the app may listen. You need to accept to play.
3. **Two seconds of quiet ("shhh…").** The app measures how your home sounds — the fan, the TV — so it does not
   mistake that noise for your child's voice. If it is very noisy, it says so openly and you decide whether to go
   on or measure again.

**The fourth, "word twins", uses no microphone**: no permission and no quiet measurement.

Tap **"We played"** whenever you like: no minimum time, no required goal, never a game over. **"Exit"** inside a
game returns you to your line screen (the microphone turns off right there) and **the game restarts fully**.

1. **The balloon — a voice that keeps going.** The balloon rises while a voice is heard and drifts down when it
   is quiet. You celebrate; the app only reports what it actually heard.
2. **The rocket — a voice that goes up and down.** The rocket follows the **pitch** of the voice: higher voice,
   higher rocket. Pitch is data about the voice and follows the same rule: never saved, never sent.
3. **Word and picture — the name lives in the thing.** You show a picture and say the word; the child answers
   however they can. The picture lights up with any sound — **that does not mean the word was right**, and the
   app never claims it was. **You** judge, and you celebrate in your own words.
4. **Word twins — two pictures that sound almost the same.** A game to play together, without a microphone.
   (Spanish only in this version; see the Language section.)

### Your family's voice — record your voice for the games

In the **Studio** you can record **your own** voice so the games sound with it. Those recordings live only on this
device. They go to no server and no repository. You can play them back, switch them off, or delete them whenever
you like. **Your child's voice is never recorded.**

### Speech stage — how to choose it

The stage sets which capsules come up. Choose the one that looks most like your child today; you can change it at
any time. "Single words" is the default level and stays the starting point.

### The path — how things are going, with no grades

**The Path** shows what you have done together by week and a few milestones. There are no grades, no ranking and
no comparison with other children. It is a mirror of your own practice, nothing more.

### The weekly goal — in tune with their therapy

Write what you are working on this week (for example, a word or a sound) and the app lines up today's capsule,
the games, and the recording batch with it, within the same stage. It previews honestly what it will change and
says plainly when nothing matches. It never forces a different stage. (In English the aligner reads Spanish
words; see the Language section.)

### The mother's document: "The pyramid, at home" — play without screens

At **`/mirada`** there is a document for the other adult in the house, with 56 activity cards grouped by the six
parts of the pyramid, written as direct instructions (Have ready · Do · Your line · Wait and see · It worked if ·
If nothing happens). It needs no screen and no setup. It is in Spanish.

### What the app measures and what it does NOT (important)

- **It does measure:** whether there is voice (sound above the noise of the house), how long it is held, and — in
  the rocket — whether the **pitch** of that voice goes up or down.
- **It does NOT measure:** which word was said, or whether it was said well. No system today assesses a young
  child's pronunciation reliably, so the app does not pretend to. In "Word and picture" the picture lights up
  with any sound.
- **You judge success.** If your child said the word, you know. Celebrate with your own words. The app never
  cheers just to cheer.

### Calm mode

One tap — in the game or in Settings — and the screen drops to dusk colours: **no meter, no goal, no automatic
celebration**. The balloon does not stop: it rises while the voice sounds and settles slowly in silence. The
rocket floats the same way. It helps when your child is overstimulated or when a game with a goal is too much.
There is also a setting to **reduce animation**, and if your system already asks for less motion, the app follows
it by itself.

### Light or dark

In Settings, **"Light or dark"** sets how **your** screen looks: follow the device ("System", the usual), or fix
it to light or dark. **The child's screen never changes: it is always light**, so the game looks the same every
day. That sameness is part of the design.

## Privacy: what happens to your child's voice

- **Your child's voice is NEVER recorded, saved, or sent off the device.** The sound is analysed on the spot,
  inside the phone or tablet, to work out two numbers — how much energy there is and, in the rocket, how high the
  pitch is — and then it is discarded. **Pitch is also data about the voice and follows the same rule.**
- **No servers, no accounts, no cloud.** During play the app makes **no** request to the internet. Automated
  tests check this on every change to the code.
- **The camera is never used.**
- **Your family's voice (YOURS) is saved — only here.** Recordings made in the Studio live on this device and
  never leave it. Even with the Studio, **your child's voice is never recorded.**
- All that is kept on the device: the nickname (if you set one), the topics, your settings, which capsules you
  completed, what you marked in "word twins", and — if you recorded them — **your** voices. Delete it all in
  **Settings → Delete my data**, or only the voice bank with **"Delete only the voice bank"**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it work without internet?** Yes, once installed. The games and capsules live inside the app.

**Does the app judge how my child speaks?** No. It reports that there was voice, and for how long. You judge the
rest.

**Can my child play alone?** No, by design. Each activity is built for an adult and a child together.

**Why does the app record my voice but never my child's?** Your child's voice is delicate data that this app
promised never to keep. Yours is under your control: you record it, play it, switch it off, or delete it.

**Does the voice I record go anywhere?** No. It stays on this device. If you lend the tablet or change device,
the recordings do not travel with you.
